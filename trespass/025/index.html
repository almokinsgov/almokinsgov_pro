Control Deck

Media Safety Controls

System media buttons use Play to keep the selected media carrier active. Previous lowers your traffic-light level one step and Next raises it one step: Green, Amber, Red. Office statuses continue refreshing whether the media carrier is playing or paused. Stop ends the media session only. Available buttons vary by phone and browser.

Connection options

This build is designed for public FeatureServer layers and does not require or store ArcGIS access tokens.

View

Manual position replaces live GPS for map direction, nearby queries, terrain sampling and AR projection. Camera and orientation sensors can still be used normally.

Clusters are visual only. Click a cluster to zoom in. At close zoom, overlapping points spread around their true location.

AR Settings

All spatial items receive their own bearing, distance and elevation and are drawn as AR anchors. Increase this when you need a denser scene.

Labels are limited separately for readability and performance. Unlabelled spatial items still remain visible as elevation-aware anchors.

Clustering and fading only affect display. Each GIS item keeps its own position, terrain height and AR projection.

3D terrain projection
m

Terrain AR uses public Terrarium raster tiles directly and does not use the rate-limited point elevation API. Large draw ranges automatically use coarser terrain tiles while retaining the selected mesh size. X-ray keeps the camera visible while showing the shape of the land.

Permission help

Phone camera, location and orientation normally require HTTPS. On iPhone, sensor permission must be requested directly from a tap.

Elevation

All built-in elevation modes are token-free. Auto samples public Terrarium tiles first and falls back to Open-Meteo when needed.

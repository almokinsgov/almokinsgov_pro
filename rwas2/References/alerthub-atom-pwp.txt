    <summary>URGENT: SAVE WATER NOW 
Kaikohe residents and businesses need to immediately reduce water use, or the town supply reservoirs will run out of water overnight.  We need everyone to act now to save water. USE WATER FOR ESSENTIAL PURPOSES ONLY. 
Do NOT wash cars or use hoses, take shorter showers, hold off washing clothes or dishes, and only flush toilets when necessary. Any action you can take to save water will help.
